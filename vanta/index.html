How it works

A simple, four-step process. You make the final call.

  1. 1

    Submit a need

    Tell us what you buy, specs, rough volumes, and where it needs to go.

  2. 2

    Evaluate suppliers

    We identify and vet potential suppliers against your requirements.

  3. 3

    Compare options

  4. 4

About Vanta

A new, hands-on sourcing partner based in Dallas–Fort Worth.

Vanta is an early-stage business. We're not a software platform or a marketplace, and we don't hold inventory. We're a small team that does the sourcing and supplier-comparison work directly.

Vanta was founded by an operator with a background in warehousing and logistics, where purchasing decisions are made under real constraints: specs that matter, freight that adds up, and deadlines that don't move.

  • Specs first. We compare like with like, and flag where options differ.
  • Delivered cost, not just unit price. Freight, minimums, and terms are part of the picture.
  • Your decision. We provide clear information; you approve the purchase.
