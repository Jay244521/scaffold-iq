FAQ

When will kits be ready?

We're assembling the first small batch now. Everyone who reserves gets a message with the pickup or delivery date before we ask for payment.

Do I pay when I reserve?

No. Reserving holds a kit from the first batch. You pay on pickup or delivery by card or cash.

Where do you deliver?

Across Dallas–Fort Worth to start, with pickup points in Dallas and Fort Worth. Shipping to the rest of Texas and the U.S. comes next.

How do I wash the towels?

Machine wash warm in the mesh bag, with no fabric softener (it clogs the fibers). Tumble dry low or hang dry. The reset card has the details.

Will it scratch my screens or paint?

Microfiber is the standard choice for screens, glass and clear-coat because it's soft and traps dirt instead of dragging it. Keep each color for its own job so grit from the floor never reaches your screens.

What if I don't like it?

Return it within 30 days for a full refund. Just message us.
