A · HORIZON

keelwell
Feel: friendly, open-road, lifestyle. Sun on the horizon over a road, with a lowercase wordmark. Best for: direct-to-consumer, social media, the pet and care lines. Reads like a modern consumer brand. Watch out: softer than a parts brand usually looks. Sun-and-road marks are common, so it needs a careful trademark search.
B · PLATE
KEELWELL AUTO PARTS & ACCESSORIES · TEXAS
KEELWELL
Feel: heritage parts-counter brand. A license-plate badge with mounting holes and a heavy wordmark. Best for: the parts line, dealer and repair-shop shelves, stamped on boxes. Closest to how established parts brands look. Watch out: the plate shape gets busy below about 20 px, and "KW" alone is a weak, common trademark. Protect the full lockup.
C · KEEL
KEELWELL PARTS · ACCESSORIES
KEELWELL
Feel: precise and engineered. A keel shape under a deck line, with a wide technical wordmark. Best for: fitted products and the Cargo System. Looks like a company that owns specifications. Strong as a deboss on rubber and plastic parts. Watch out: cooler and less friendly for the pet line. The wide wordmark needs room on narrow packs like wiper boxes.
Current shield (logo sheet): the middle ground. Sturdy enough for parts, friendly enough for care and pet. If parts matter most: B or C. If the first year is all direct-to-consumer: A or the current shield. Any choice: run the trademark search on the logo as well as the name, and ask a designer to refine the final pick into production files.
