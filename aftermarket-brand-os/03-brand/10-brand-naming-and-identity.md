# 10 — Brand Naming and Identity Package

> **No name below has been cleared.** A quick web search found *no obvious automotive conflict* for a few of them. That is **not** a trademark search. One name the team considered, "Roamstead", was dropped because a pending federal application (Class 43, hospitality) uses it. Follow the screening process before choosing anything.

## 1. Structure
- **Parent company** (legal entity, later an LLC): a neutral, durable name, e.g. "[Surname] Brands" or "[Name] Holdings". Customers never see it.
- **One consumer brand** for now, covering three product families: **Care** (cleaning), **Pet** (pet travel protection) and **Cargo** (organization and protection). Add a second brand only when there is a clear commercial reason (e.g. a premium fitted line or a wholesale-only label).

## 3. Trademark and domain screening process ($0 until filing)
1. **Knockout search, free:** search [USPTO Trademark Search](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/) for exact and similar spellings, phonetic variants and plurals. Focus on:
   - **Class 12:** vehicle accessories, including fitted seat covers and cargo liners
   - **Class 21:** cleaning cloths and brushes
   - **Class 18:** bags
   - **Class 35:** retail store services
2. **Common-law check:** Google, Amazon, Etsy, Instagram and TikTok for unregistered users in auto or pet.
3. **Texas check:** [Texas SOS](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/) business name search (SOSDirect) and Dallas/Tarrant county assumed-name records.
4. **Domain:** look for `.com` first; check registrars for price. **Do not buy until founder approval.**
5. **Social handles:** the same handle on Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook and X.
6. **Decision:** pick 1 primary name and 1 alternate. Before spending $350+ per class on a USPTO filing, consider a flat-fee attorney clearance opinion (founder approval needed; this is outside the $300 phase).
7. **Filing (post-$300 phase):** a USPTO base application costs $350 per class [L5]. Use the ID Manual wording to avoid the $200 free-form surcharge. Prioritise Class 12 and Class 21. A filed application is the gateway to Amazon Brand Registry (sources conflict on whether pending applications are accepted; confirm with Amazon).

## 4. Positioning
- **For** everyday sedan, crossover and SUV owners (and their dogs) **who** want their vehicle to stay clean, protected and organised without becoming car-care hobbyists, **[Brand] is** a vehicle-interior care and protection brand **that** delivers products that fit, last in Texas heat and come with clear guidance. **Unlike** generic marketplace brands, we publish fit guides, test in real Texas conditions and accept test-fit returns.
- **Mission:** keep every vehicle feeling new for longer, simply.
- **Brand promise:** *Fits right. Built for heat. Easy to return.*
- **Personality:** capable, straightforward, friendly, quietly premium. Think of a good service advisor, not a hype brand.
- **Tagline candidates:** "Keep it like new." · "Ready for every road." · "Built for real life in your car."

## 6. Visual identity direction
- **Feel:** a national aftermarket company with engineering credibility, not a dropshipping store.
- **Logo concepts** (for a freelancer or a designer friend later; no agency):
  1. **Wordmark:** strong geometric sans-serif in all caps, with a custom cut on one letter echoing a road line.
  2. **Monogram badge:** the brand initial inside a rounded shield, reading like a vehicle emblem and embroiderable on towels.
  3. **Horizon mark:** a simple line forming a road and horizon above the wordmark, usable as a single-colour deboss on rubber tools.
- **Colour palette:**
  - Primary **Graphite** #1F2A36 (premium, hides dirt in photos)
  - Accent **Signal Orange** #E8671B (energy; visibility on Amazon thumbnails)
  - Supporting **Sand** #E9E2D6 (Texas, warmth)
  - **Steel** #8A97A5
  - **White** #FFFFFF
- **Typography:** a geometric/grotesk sans for headlines (e.g. Inter Tight, Montserrat or Barlow Condensed, all free Google Fonts) and Inter for body text. Free fonts keep the cost at $0.
- **Packaging direction:** kraft or graphite box with one-colour orange print (cheap to print, premium feel). A large pictogram of contents on the front. A **"Fits right. Built for heat."** promise strip. A QR code to the how-to video. Recyclable materials. Same layout system across every SKU, so the catalog looks like a family on a shelf.
- **Labelling strategy:**
  - Product name is consistent: `[Brand] Care · Microfiber Kit 9-pc`.
  - Every label lists the **fit or size** first, then contents.
  - Legally required items: fibre content and origin for textiles, the company identity, and warnings.
  - FNSKU barcode placement follows Amazon requirements.

## 8. Messaging pillars
1. **Fit:** "Measured on real vehicles. Fit guide for every product."
2. **Durability:** "Tested in Texas heat." Only claim this after we actually run heat tests and document them.
3. **Simplicity:** "One kit. Fifteen minutes. Clean car."
4. **Service:** "Test-fit it. If it's not right, send it back."

## 9. Social media identity
- **Handle:** @[brand] or @[brand]auto, the same on every platform.
- **Content pillars:**
  1. 15-second before/after interior resets (DFW cars, real owners)
  2. Dog-car life
  3. "Fit check" videos by vehicle
  4. Texas-heat tests
  5. Behind the brand (sample testing, supplier choices)
- **Voice:** helpful and plain-spoken. No fake scarcity or exaggerated claims.
