# 12 — Customer Validation Questionnaire

**Goal:** confirm that DFW sedan, crossover and SUV owners will **pay** for the first product, and learn which variant and price they choose.

- **Sample:** at least 15 interviews (8 or more dog owners) plus a short online survey (target 50 responses).
- **Cost:** $0–$10. Use free Google Forms. A coffee or a sample towel is the only thank-you.

## Interview rules
1. Ask about **past behaviour**, not hypothetical wishes. "When did you last…" beats "Would you…".
2. Don't pitch until the end. Don't lead the witness.
3. Ask permission to record or take notes. Collect no personal data beyond first name and an optional email for follow-up (with consent).
4. Log every interview in the table at the bottom within 2 hours.

## Part A — Screening (30 sec)
1. What do you drive (make, model, year)?
2. Is it your main daily vehicle? About how many minutes a day do you drive?
3. Do any pets ride in it? What kind and size?

## Part B — Current behaviour (5–8 min)
4. Walk me through the last time you cleaned the inside of your car. When was it, what did you use, and how long did it take?
5. Where did those products come from? About what did you spend?
6. What annoys you most about the inside of your car right now?
7. *(Dog owners)* What happens to your car after a trip with your dog? What have you tried? What failed, and why?
8. Have you bought any car accessory online in the last 12 months? Which one, where, and why that one?
9. Have you returned a car accessory? What happened?

## Part C — Problem ranking (2 min)
10. Rank these from most to least annoying:
    - Dust and grime on the dash and console
    - Pet hair
    - Trunk/cargo clutter
    - Spills and stains
    - Scratched cargo area or bumper
    - Interior heat/sun damage

## Part D — Concept test (show a sample or photo)
11. *(Show Care Kit)* What do you think this is for? Who is it for?
12. What would you expect to pay? *(Let them answer first, then ask:)* At what price is it too expensive to consider? At what price is it so cheap you'd doubt the quality?
13. Which would you pick?
    - (a) Care Kit, 9 pieces, $29.99
    - (b) Pet Owner Kit (+ pet-hair tool), $36.99
    - (c) Neither
14. *(Dog owners, show a liner photo)* What would make you trust a cargo liner to fit your vehicle? What price feels right?
15. Where would you expect to buy this? (Amazon / brand site / store / local seller)

## Part E — Commitment test (the real signal)
16. "We're making a first small batch in [month]. Would you like to pre-order one at the launch price of $[X]? You'd pay on pickup or delivery."
    - Record **Yes-with-payment / Yes-later / No**.
    - A pre-order or deposit counts as validation. A compliment does not.
17. May we follow up when samples arrive? (optional email or phone, with consent)

## Online survey version (Google Forms, 10 questions)
Use questions 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 10, 12, 13, 15 and 16 (as "Join the launch list"), with multiple-choice answers where possible.

## Success criteria (decide before collecting data)
| Signal | Threshold to proceed with Care Kit | Threshold that switches to backup (Pet Liner) |
|---|---|---|
| Problem ranking | "Dust/grime" or "pet hair" in top 2 for ≥ 60% | "Pet hair/scratched cargo" top 1 for ≥ 50% of dog owners |
| Median "expected price" | ≥ $25 | Liner ≥ $50 |
| Pre-orders / deposits | ≥ 8 from ≥ 15 interviews + survey list | ≥ 5 liner deposits *and* a supplier offering ≤ 100 MOQ |
| Preferred channel | Note the split (it drives the channel plan) | |
