# 11 — Product Specification Templates

A spec sheet is how we come to **own the product definition**, even when the manufacturer owns the tooling. Version every sheet, and attach the approved one to every purchase order.

## Filled example — Microfiber Care Kit v0.1 (draft, to be confirmed by samples)
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Contents | 6 × all-purpose 16×16 in (40×40 cm), 300–350 gsm, edgeless or silk-banded, 3 colours × 2 · 1 × drying towel 20×30 in, twisted loop ≥1,000 gsm · 2 × waffle glass towel 16×16 in · 1 × mesh wash bag · 1 × guide card |
| Fibre | 70/30 or 80/20 polyester/polyamide (state on label) |
| Performance | No lint on glass after 1 wipe; no colour bleed in first wash at 40 °C; edges don't scratch clear-coat (rub test on a black panel) |
| Avoid | Hard sewn edges on paint towels; dye bleed; thin "400 gsm claimed, 250 actual" (weigh samples) |
| Logo | Woven label, corner, on 1 of every colour set (cost-dependent) |
| Packaging | Kraft mailer box 10×8×3 in (A), 1-colour print; FNSKU label outside |
| Inspection | Count, size ±1 cm, GSM by weighing (±10%), colour match, stitching, label, packaging print |

## Filled example — Pet Cargo Liner v0.1 (backup)
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
