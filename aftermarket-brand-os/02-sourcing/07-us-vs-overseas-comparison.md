# 07 — U.S. vs. Overseas Manufacturing Comparison

| Factor | U.S. wholesale / distributor | U.S. contract manufacturer (cut-and-sew) | China OEM/ODM | Pakistan / India / Vietnam OEM |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Minimum order | **None to one case** (e.g. S01 lists no domestic minimum) | Usually 100s of units; prototypes possible (S25) | 100–2,000 typical (listings: 100, 300, 500, 1,000, 2,000) | Similar to China; "flexible" claims |
| Unit cost | Higher (the distributor's margin is included) | Highest (U.S. labour) | Lowest FOB | Low FOB |
| Duty (examples) | Already paid by the importer, built into the price | None | Microfiber about **12.8%**; organizers (4202) about **42.6%** [T5, T6]; plus a possible unverified extra 12.5% [T3] | MFN only: microfiber 5.3%, 4202 bags 17.6% (verify per HTS and origin) |
| Lead time | Days | 2–6 weeks (A) | 30–45 days production plus 25–40 days sea freight (A) | Similar |
| Customisation | Labels and printing only | Full (our patterns) | Full (logo, packaging, spec changes) | Full |
| Quality control | Inspect on arrival; easy returns | Visit the shop; easy iteration | Needs pre-shipment inspection (third-party QC typically costs a few hundred dollars per inspection, A) | Same |
| Cash tied up | Low; restock monthly | Medium | **High**: deposit (often 30%) plus balance before shipment, about 3 months of cover | High |
| Communication / IP risk | Low | Low | Medium; use an NNN (non-use, non-disclosure, non-circumvention) agreement for custom designs | Medium |
| "Made in USA" claim | **No.** The distributor's goods are probably imported | Only if "all or virtually all" is U.S.-made (FTC standard). Get counsel review | No | No |
| Best use for us | **Validation (now)** | **Flagship prototypes (Stage 2–3)** | **Scale for products with low duty** | **Microfiber and 4202 goods at scale (avoids Section 301)** |

## Recommendation
1. **Now ($300 phase):** buy from **U.S. wholesalers** in case-size or smaller quantities. Margin is lower, but there is no MOQ, no import paperwork and no duty risk. This is the only option that fits the budget.
2. **After validation (~25+ orders/month sustained):** request samples from **one China and one Pakistan microfiber factory**. Moving production overseas lifts kit contribution from about $7.92 to about $10.04 per own-site order (model).
3. **Backup and pet line:** source overseas. China is acceptable if the HTS duty is moderate. Always get the customs broker's HTS determination first.
4. **Organizers and bags (HTS 4202):** avoid China origin while the 25% Section 301 duty stands. Ask the same suppliers whether they produce in Vietnam or Cambodia.
5. **Flagship system:** prototype in the U.S. (fast iteration, patterns we own), then tender the production run.
