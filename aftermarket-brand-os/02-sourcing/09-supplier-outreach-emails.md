# 09 — Supplier Outreach Email Templates

Rules:
- Keep messages short.
- One RFQ per supplier.
- Never promise volumes we can't back.
- Never send payment before the qualification checklist ([`06-supplier-database.md`](06-supplier-database.md)) is complete **and** the founder has approved the spend.

---

### A. First contact — overseas manufacturer
**Subject:** RFQ: private-label [microfiber detailing kit] — U.S. brand, samples requested

Hello [Name/Team],

I'm the founder of [Brand], a new automotive accessories brand based in Dallas, Texas. We found your [product] on [website/Alibaba] and would like a quotation for a private-label version.

Our RFQ is attached. In short: [6+1+2 towel kit, logo label, retail packaging], quotes at 100 / 300 / 500 / 1,000 units, plus sample cost to Dallas.

Could you also confirm:
1. Are you the manufacturer or a trading company?
2. What is the MOQ for this exact configuration with our logo and packaging?
3. Can you share your business licence and any test or audit reports?

We're comparing a small number of suppliers and plan to decide within two weeks of receiving samples.

Best regards,
[Name] — Founder, [Brand] · [email]

---

### B. First contact — U.S. wholesaler
**Subject:** Wholesale / private-label inquiry — new Texas auto accessories brand

Hi [Name],

I'm launching [Brand], a car-care and interior accessories brand in Dallas–Fort Worth. I'm interested in your [16×16 300gsm towels / private-label towels] for a branded care kit.

Could you tell me:
1. Your sample options and cost
2. Pricing for drying towels and glass/waffle towels
3. Where the towels are manufactured
4. The minimum for custom logo labels
5. Typical ship time to Texas
6. Whether you offer reseller or distributor terms

Thank you,
[Name] · [Brand] · [email]

---

### C. Follow-up (no reply after 4 business days)
**Subject:** Re: RFQ [product] — quick follow-up

Hi [Name], just following up on the RFQ below. Even a quick MOQ and sample price would help us decide on our shortlist this week. Thanks! — [Name]

---

### D. Sample request after a good quote (only after founder approval)
**Subject:** Sample order — [product] — [Brand]

Hi [Name], thank you for the quote. We'd like to order **one sample** of [configuration] at the quoted [$X sample + $Y courier]. Please send a payment link through [platform with buyer protection] and confirm the ship date and tracking. Please include a photo of the carton label and the sample's tag before shipping.

---

### E. Polite decline (keep the door open)
**Subject:** Re: RFQ [product]

Hi [Name], thank you for your quick and detailed quote. We've chosen a different configuration for our first run, but we'd like to keep your details on file for future products. Best — [Name]

---

### F. Negotiation — lower MOQ for a trial order
**Subject:** Trial order request — [product]

Hi [Name], your sample quality is our top choice. Because we're a new brand, could you accept a **trial order of [100] units** at [price], or offer a stock-colour version with only our label at a lower minimum? If the trial sells through, we'd move to your [500]-unit tier for the reorder. — [Name]

---

[Name] · [phone] · [email]
