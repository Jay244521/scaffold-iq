## Supplier qualification checklist (complete before any sample payment)
1. **Legal identity:** business licence name matches the bank beneficiary name. Check Alibaba "Verified" status or an equivalent third-party verification where applicable.
2. **Factory or trader?** Ask for a live video walk-through of the production line and the warehouse.
3. **Years in business; export markets; U.S. customers** they can name, or anonymised references.
4. **Product-specific MOQ** for *our* configuration (colour, size, logo, packaging), in writing.
5. **Test reports:** for textiles, GSM, colourfastness and composition. For PVC/vinyl, phthalates and Prop 65 chemicals. Audit reports (BSCI, ISO 9001) if available.
6. **Payment terms:** never pay sample fees to a personal account. Prefer platform protection (e.g. Alibaba Trade Assurance) or a card payment.
7. **Score each supplier** on price, MOQ, responsiveness (<48 h), sample quality and communication. Pick at least 2 per product so we never depend on one source.
