# 17 — Legal and Compliance Checklist

> This is a research checklist, **not legal, tax or customs advice.** Items marked **[Pro]** need a licensed professional: a Texas business attorney, a CPA, or a licensed customs broker. Free help: the Small Business Development Centers (SBDC) at Dallas-area universities and SCORE mentors offer free counselling (check current availability).

## A. Business setup
| ✓ | Item | Detail | Cost | When |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ☐ | **Structure decision** [Pro advisable] | Start as a **sole proprietor + DBA** (cheapest; low-risk first product). Form a **Texas LLC** ($300 Form 205 [L1]) at gate G1 or **before selling higher-liability products** (pet liners, fitted products). An LLC does not replace insurance | $0 now | Day 1 decision |
| ☐ | **Assumed name certificate (DBA)** | Required in Texas when trading under a name other than your own. File with the county clerk where you do business (Dallas County ~$23–$24, notarised [L4]; other DFW counties differ). An LLC trading under a brand name files its assumed name with the Secretary of State | ~$25 | Week 2 (after name screening) |
| ☐ | **EIN** | Free from the IRS. Lets you avoid using your SSN with suppliers and marketplaces | $0 | Week 2 |
| ☐ | **Business bank account** | Keep business money separate from personal money | $0 (no-fee account) | Week 2 |
| ☐ | **Bookkeeping** | Spreadsheet → free tier tool. Keep receipts 4+ years | $0 | Day 1 |
| ☐ | **Local home-business rules** | Check your city's home-occupation rules if storing inventory or receiving pickups at home | $0 | Week 1 |

## B. Tax
| ✓ | Item | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| ☐ | **Texas sales and use tax permit** | Free from the Comptroller. **Required for a Texas-based seller even when selling only through marketplaces that collect tax** [L3]. Collect tax on local and own-site Texas sales; file on the assigned schedule |
| ☐ | **Resale certificate** | Texas Form 01-339 lets you buy inventory for resale without paying sales tax. Give it to U.S. wholesalers (S01, S02) |
| ☐ | **Marketplace facilitator** | Amazon, eBay and Walmart collect and remit for marketplace sales. Keep their certification records [L3] |
| ☐ | **Other states** | Economic-nexus thresholds apply to own-site sales into other states. Watch them as you scale [Pro] |
| ☐ | **Federal income tax** | Schedule C as a sole proprietor; quarterly estimates once profitable [Pro: CPA] |
| ☐ | **Texas franchise tax** | Applies to LLCs. There is a no-tax-due revenue threshold, but annual reports are still required [Pro] |

## C. Intellectual property
| ✓ | Item | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| ☐ | Trademark knockout search | USPTO search + common law (see brand package) |
| ☐ | **USPTO application** | $350 per class base fee [L5]. Classes 12 and 21 first. Use ID Manual wording. Consider an attorney clearance opinion [Pro] |
| ☐ | Domain and handles | Register after the knockout search |
| ☐ | **Freedom to operate** | Google Patents search for pet-hair tools, cargo-liner attachment designs and organizers before ordering. Do not copy patented or trade-dress designs [Pro for anything non-generic] |
| ☐ | **Supplier NNN agreement** | Non-use, non-disclosure, non-circumvention for custom designs, enforceable in the supplier's country [Pro] |
| ☐ | Own artwork | Get written assignment of copyright from any designer or freelancer |

## D. Supplier and private-label agreements [Pro for the template]
Must include:
- Specifications and the golden sample
- Price and MOQ
- Lead time and late-delivery remedy
- Quality / AQL and inspection rights
- Defect remedy (replace or credit)
- IP ownership of our logo, artwork and specs
- **No selling our branded version to others**
- Compliance warranties (restricted substances, Prop 65, forced-labour-free supply chain)
- Payment terms
- Governing law and dispute venue
- Product-liability indemnity and supplier insurance where obtainable

## E. Product safety, labelling and liability
| ✓ | Item | Applies to | Detail |
|---|---|---|---|
| ☐ | **Product liability insurance** | All products; essential for liners, fitted goods, dealer/retail accounts | Get quotes at gate G1–G3 (often required by Amazon above a sales threshold and by retailers or dealers) [Pro: broker] |
| ☐ | **Textile labelling** | Towels, liners, organizers | FTC Textile Act: fibre content, country of origin, dealer identity (RN number is free from the FTC). **Confirm whether cleaning cloths are covered or exempt** [Pro] |
| ☐ | **California Prop 65** | Anything with PVC, vinyl, rubber or coated fabrics (pet liners, organizers, mats) | Specify non-PVC/phthalate-free materials and require test reports. If a listed chemical is present above safe-harbour levels, a warning is required. Enforcement actions regularly name Amazon-sold PVC goods [L6] |
| ☐ | **CPSIA / children's products** | Only if marketed for children (e.g. a kids' back-seat organizer) | Children's products need third-party testing and a Children's Product Certificate. **Avoid marketing to children** until ready |
| ☐ | **Vehicle safety interference** | Seat covers, hammocks, phone mounts | Must not block side airbags, seat belts, child-seat anchors or the driver's view. Add warnings. Several such products are on the "avoid" list for this reason |
| ☐ | **FMVSS** | Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standards apply mainly to vehicles and certain equipment (e.g. lighting, tyres, glazing). **Our interior soft goods are generally not FMVSS-regulated**, but anything touching lighting, glazing, restraints or visibility could be [Pro check before such products] |
| ☐ | **Poly-bag suffocation warnings** | Packaging | Several states require warnings on large poly bags |
| ☐ | Recordkeeping | All | Keep spec sheets, test reports, lot numbers and supplier invoices to support any recall |

## F. Import and customs
| ✓ | Item | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| ☐ | **HTS classification per product** [Pro: customs broker] | Microfiber 6307.10 (~5.3% + 7.5% Section 301 if China [T5]); organizers 4202.92 (17.6% + 25% Section 301 if China [T6]); confirm each |
| ☐ | **Current tariff layers** | IEEPA tariffs struck down Feb-2026 [T1]; Section 122 expired Jul-2026 [T2]; Section 301 continues [T3]. **Re-check before every order.** Reports of an extra 12.5% Section 301 duty are unverified |
| ☐ | **No de minimis** | Every commercial import pays duty, including small ones [T4] |
| ☐ | **Importer of record** | DDP terms (supplier is importer) vs us as importer (needs a customs bond for formal entries, and a broker) |
| ☐ | **Country-of-origin marking** | Goods must be marked with origin ("Made in China") |
| ☐ | **Forced-labour compliance** | The Uyghur Forced Labor Prevention Act presumes goods with Xinjiang inputs are barred. **Cotton and polyester supply chains are high-risk.** Ask suppliers for material origin declarations [Pro] |
| ☐ | **FDA/EPA** | Not applicable to dry towels and soft goods. Chemicals (sprays, cleaners) bring EPA, DOT and SDS obligations, **one reason the first kit excludes chemicals** |

## G. Consumer protection and marketplaces
| ✓ | Item | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| ☐ | Website policies | Privacy, terms, shipping, returns and warranty pages |
| ☐ | **Warranty wording** | Magnuson-Moss rules apply to written warranties on consumer products [Pro review] |
| ☐ | Advertising claims | Substantiate every claim. "Made in USA" only under the FTC standard. Endorsement disclosures |
| ☐ | **Reviews** | No incentivised, fake or gated reviews (FTC rule, 2024; Amazon policy) |
| ☐ | Texas Deceptive Trade Practices Act | Honest pricing (no fake "was" prices), accurate descriptions |
| ☐ | **Marketplace seller requirements** | Amazon: identity verification, GTIN/UPC (buy from GS1 US, not resellers), category approvals if any, Prop 65 compliance, insurance above thresholds. eBay: business account, returns policy |
| ☐ | INFORM Consumers Act | High-volume third-party sellers must give marketplaces verified identity information |

## H. Professional advice — when to pay for it
| Advisor | When | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Customs broker | Before the first overseas order | HTS, duty, entry filing |
| Trademark attorney | Before filing (gate G1) | Clearance and class strategy |
| Business attorney | Before the LLC, supplier contracts and the warranty | Contracts and liability |
| Insurance broker | At gate G1–G3 | Product-liability quotes |
| CPA | After first revenue | Sales tax, Schedule C, entity choice |
