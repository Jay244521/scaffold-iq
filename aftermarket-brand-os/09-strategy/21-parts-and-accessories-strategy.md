# 21 — Parts + Accessories Strategy (the full RealTruck model)

RealTruck runs **parts and accessories**. It owns more than two dozen in-house brands (BAK, Retrax, TruXedo, Extang, UnderCover, AMP Research, A.R.E., OMIX and others) and sells everything by vehicle, from tonneau covers and bumpers to lighting, suspension and Jeep parts. [RealTruck brands](https://realtruck.com/our-brands) · [RealTruck press release](https://prnewswire.com/news-releases/realtruck-reaches-million-part-milestone-with-new-products-301506692.html)

Our version: **one brand selling accessories and parts for sedans, crossovers and SUVs, all organized by vehicle.** We add parts in order of risk, so the company never takes on a liability it can't carry.

## 1. Parts sorted by risk
| Tier | What | Examples | Why this tier | When |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1: wear parts with low liability** | Replaced regularly; failure is an annoyance, not a crash | **Wiper blades, cabin air filters**, interior LED bulbs, trim clips and fastener kits, key-fob shells | Repeat purchases; light; drive traffic to the fit finder | **Stage 2** (after gate G2) |
| **Tier 2: fit-critical parts with moderate liability** | Wrong fit causes damage or returns | Engine air filters, splash guards/mud flaps, fitted floor and cargo liners, OEM-style trim | Needs fitment data and quality control; some need tooling | **Stage 3** |
| **Tier 3: regulated or safety-critical** | Failure can injure someone, or a federal standard applies | **Brakes, suspension, steering**, exterior lighting (FMVSS 108/DOT), mirrors, glass, anything touching emissions (EPA/CARB anti-tampering) | High liability, certification and insurance demands | **Not before Stage 4**, and only with legal, insurance and engineering support, or by acquiring a brand that already has them |

The scores in the workbook show this split. Cabin filters (#6) and wipers (#12) rank with the stronger accessories. Exterior LED headlight bulbs (#20) and brakes (#26) land near the bottom because of their legal and safety risk.

## 2. The first two parts lines
### Wiper blades (parts line #1)
- **Why first:** every car needs new blades every 6–12 months. Beam blades with multi-adapter kits cover most vehicles with about 8–10 sizes (14–28 in), which keeps the number of SKUs low. They are light and cheap to ship.
- **Suppliers seen (unverified):**
  - Xiamen Meto: OEM/ODM, one listing shows a 600-unit MOQ
  - DR Auto Parts (Ningbo): makes beam blades
  - Ostar (Xiamen): OEM blades
  - An Alibaba seller blog puts typical MOQs at 200–500 units, possibly lower for private label

  Sources: [Gateway Lines](https://gatewaylines.com/suppliers/xiamen-meto-auto-parts-industry), [SourceReady](https://www.sourceready.com/supplier/detail/dr-auto-parts-co-ltd), [Alibaba seller blog](https://seller.alibaba.com/blogs/2026/southeast-asia/auto-parts/silicone-beam-wiper-blade-fleet-procurement-guide-alibaba-b2b)
- **Capital (assumption):** about 8 sizes × 200–600 units is a large first order. Negotiate a mixed-size first order, or start with the 4–5 sizes that fit the most DFW vehicles.
- **Fit:** publish a driver-side and passenger-side size chart by vehicle. Every chart entry becomes a fitment record.

### Cabin air filters (parts line #2)
- **Why:** replaced every 12,000–15,000 miles. It's a simple job customers can do in 5 minutes. A Texas angle works here (pollen, dust, summer AC use) and pairs naturally with the Care Kit as an "interior air and surfaces" message.
- **Suppliers seen (unverified):**
  - Buket: 200–500 MOQ per model; private labeling, custom packaging and custom part-number printing
  - A Global Sources listing: 500 units, 35–45 days, customizable box
  - Premium Guard and APC Filtration: private-label programs
  - Newline Filters: OEM/ODM

  Sources: [Buket](https://www.buketautoparts.com/product-category/automotive-filters/), [Global Sources](https://www.globalsources.com/Cabin-filter/Auto-filters-1189307831p.htm), [Premium Guard](https://premiumguard.com/?p=12743), [APC](https://www.apcfilters.com/?p=1111)
- **Catch:** each MOQ applies **per part number**. Covering the top 10 DFW vehicles could mean 10 part numbers × 200–500 units. Start with the 3–5 part numbers that cover the most popular local models (several models share a filter; confirm this with fitment data).

## 3. What parts change about the business
| Area | Accessories-only | With parts |
|---|---|---|
| Shopping | Browse by category | **Shop by year/make/model is mandatory.** Wrong-part returns are the #1 cost |
| Data | A simple fit guide | **Fitment data.** The industry standard is ACES (fitment) and PIES (product attributes) from the Auto Care Association. One vendor quotes ACES access at about $1,050–$10,868 a year by company size (unverified). Low-cost options: use the supplier's own cross-reference data, eBay compatibility charts and Amazon's fitment tools until volume justifies a licence. [Auto Care FAQ](https://autocare.org/about-us/frequently-asked-questions/details/what-is-aces-and-pies-data) · [Credencys](https://www.credencys.com/blog/managing-product-data-auto-parts-aces-pies) |
| SKUs and capital | A few SKUs | Many SKUs (one per vehicle or size). Capital and storage grow faster, so the cash-trap warning in the financial model gets stronger |
| Channels | Social, DTC | Parts buyers search by part number and vehicle on Amazon, eBay Motors (compatibility charts) and Google. Repair shops and installers become customers |
| Legal | Low liability | Product liability insurance before any parts launch; never use OEM trademarks except to describe fit ("fits Toyota RAV4"); no claims of OEM equivalence without test data; FMVSS/EPA screening for every new part |
| Moat | Brand and service | **Fitment database plus part-number cross-reference.** This is the moat behind RealTruck and the big parts brands |

## 5. Why parts don't replace the Care Kit as the first product
Every parts line has an MOQ per size or per vehicle (200–600 units each) and needs fitment data to sell without heavy returns. Neither fits a $300 start. The Care Kit pays for the samples, the data and the first parts order. Parts then turn the site into somewhere customers come back to (every 6–12 months for wipers and filters), which accessories alone don't do.
