## Why the allocation changed
1. **Added the DBA (~$25).** Texas requires an assumed name certificate when a sole proprietor trades under a name other than their own. It is also usually needed to open a bank account in the brand's name. The original budget missed it.
2. **Moved money from "samples" to a micro-batch that only pre-orders unlock.** Selling 12 real kits teaches far more than holding 6 samples, and paid pre-orders fund most of it.
3. **Skipped the $300 Texas LLC for now.** The filing fee alone [L1] would consume the whole budget. Start as a **sole proprietor with a DBA**. This is reasonable for low-risk microfiber products. Form the LLC at gate G1. Get legal advice if you plan to sell higher-risk products before then.
4. **No subscriptions in this phase:**
   - Square Online free plan (or similar) instead of Shopify at $39/month
   - eBay free listings instead of Amazon Pro at $39.99/month
   - Amazon Individual at $0.99/item, only if needed
