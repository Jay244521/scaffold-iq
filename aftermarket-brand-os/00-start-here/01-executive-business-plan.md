## 1. Opportunity
- The U.S. specialty-equipment aftermarket is about **$52.9B (2025)**, growing about 1% a year [M1]. Trucks and SUVs account for about 43% of spending [M2], and about 56% of purchases start online [M3].
- The biggest multi-brand house, **RealTruck**, is truck-focused and was financially stressed in 2025 [C6].
- The everyday **sedan, crossover and SUV owner**, especially the **dog owner**, is served mostly by single-product Amazon brands (FORTEM, 4Knines, Drop Stop) and by premium floor-liner makers (WeatherTech, TuxMat). Nobody owns *interior care and protection that fits, survives heat and is easy to return*.

## 2. Strategy
Build a private-label **brand house** in four stages:
1. **Private label:** existing products, our brand.
2. **Customised:** our specs.
3. **Proprietary:** our patterns, fit data and designs.
4. **Platform:** wholesale, dealers, installers, acquisitions.

Start with one consumer brand and three product families: **Care, Pet and Cargo**.

## 3. First product and sequence
1. **Microfiber Care Kit** (first). No MOQ via U.S. wholesale, very low risk, about 63% contribution on local sales. See [`19-first-product-recommendation.md`](19-first-product-recommendation.md).
2. Pet-hair tool and brush add-ons, to raise order value.
3. **Pet cargo liner with integrated bumper flap** (backup and first spec-owned product). Best unit economics: $18.45 per own-site order.
4. Back-seat pet hammock.
5. **SUV Cargo Protection System** (flagship, proprietary fit).

## 4. Go-to-market
- **Days 1–30:** local DFW direct sales (pre-orders, car meets, Marketplace, dog community) and a free storefront.
- **Day 24:** eBay.
- **Gates G1–G3:** Amazon (Professional plan and Brand Registry), after a trademark filing and about 40 units/month.
- **From week 3:** B2B with detailers and car washes.
- **After G1:** dealerships.

## 5. Economics (scenarios, not forecasts)
| Orders/month | 10 | 25 | 50 | 100 | 500 | 1,000 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue / month | $300 | $666 | $1,450 | $3,611 | $18,770 | $37,052 |
| Operating profit / month (pre-tax) | $142 | $286 | $358 | $926 | $3,310 | $5,509 |
| Inventory cash needed | $90 | $226 | $772 | $2,787 | $15,438 | $31,718 |

**Key insight:** inventory needs outpace profit, so growth is cash-limited. A reinvestment rule (50% inventory / 15% marketing / 15% product development / 15% reserve / 5% founder draw) and gated milestones (G1–G5) control the pace. See [`../05-finance/13-financial-model.md`](../05-finance/13-financial-model.md).
