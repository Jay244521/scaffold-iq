# 19 — First-Product Recommendation

## Decision
- **First product: Microfiber Care Kit** (9 pieces, color-coded, with a "15-Minute Interior Reset" guide). Sourced from a **U.S. wholesaler** for validation.
- **Backup: Pet Cargo Liner with integrated bumper flap**, built to our spec.
- **Flagship to develop:** the Complete SUV Cargo Protection System (Stage 2–3).

## Head-to-head on the brief's criteria
| Criterion | Microfiber Care Kit | Pet cargo liner | Trunk organizer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Low startup cost | ✅ **~$60–$150 to stock 12–15 kits** | ❌ ~$2.7k–$4.7k (300 MOQ) | ❌ ~$700–$1.3k (100 MOQ at $6.90) + 42.6% duty |
| Small MOQ | ✅ No minimum (U.S. wholesaler listing) | ⚠️ ~300 typical; trial orders must be negotiated | ⚠️ 100–1,000 |
| Low shipping cost | ✅ Light, flat | ❌ Bulky (FBA ~$8.50, A) | ❌ Bulky |
| Strong demand | ✅ Evergreen, consumable | ✅ Strong, pet niche | ✅ Strong, but dominated by FORTEM (51k reviews) |
| Simple fulfillment | ✅ No fitment | ❌ Sizing returns | ⚠️ |
| Contribution margin | ⚠️ $7.92 own site, $5.59 Amazon, **$18.92 local** | ✅ **$18.45 own site, $11.99 Amazon** | ❌ $6.55 / $4.31 |
| Bundling | ✅ Pet tool, brushes, refills, liner | ✅ Care Kit, hammock, flap | ⚠️ |
| Liability / regulatory | ✅ Very low | ⚠️ Prop 65 (PVC), fit | ✅ Low |

## Why the Care Kit wins as the *first* product
It is the **only** option that can be stocked, tested, photographed, sold and delivered inside $300, with no MOQ and no import. It turns pre-orders into inventory almost cash-neutrally. It earns about 63% contribution on local sales. And it builds the brand, channels and customer list the liner launch will need.

## What is weak about it (stated plainly)
- **Commodity product.** Differentiation comes from the system, guide and service, not the towel. Expect copycats.
- **Thin Amazon margin at U.S. wholesale cost** (~19%). It improves to ~26% with overseas sourcing after validation.

It is the right *first* product, not the company's best long-term product.

## When to switch to the backup instead
Switch if **all** of these happen during the 30-day validation:
1. Interviews show pet-owner pain clearly beats general cleaning pain (≥50% of dog owners rank hair or cargo damage #1).
2. At least 5 liner deposits are collected.
3. A qualified supplier accepts a **≤100-unit trial** at a landed cost that keeps contribution positive.

Even then, the Care Kit can continue as a low-effort add-on.
