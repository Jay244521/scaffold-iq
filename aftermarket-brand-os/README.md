# Aftermarket Brand OS

The operating folder for a bootstrapped U.S. automotive-aftermarket private-label brand: $300 start, Dallas–Fort Worth, sedan, crossover and SUV owners. Prepared 2026-11-13.

> ⚠️ **No purchases, deposits, sample orders, subscriptions, ads or other commitments are authorised.** Every spend line waits for the founder's written approval in the Budget Tracker.
>
> 📎 **Evidence:** every factual claim carries a source ID from [`01-research/sources.md`](01-research/sources.md) and a grade:
> - V = primary source
> - S = secondary source
> - L = listing
> - A = assumption
>
> Supplier prices and MOQs are listings or assumptions, **not quotes**.

## Bottom line
- **First product:** Microfiber Care Kit, sourced from a U.S. wholesaler with no minimum order.
- **Backup:** Pet cargo liner with integrated bumper flap. Best unit economics, but needs about $2.7k+ for a first order.
- **Flagship to build toward:** Complete SUV Cargo Protection System.
- **First channel:** local DFW direct sales plus a free storefront. eBay next. Amazon after the trademark filing.
- **Parts + accessories (RealTruck model):** add wear parts with low liability (wiper blades, then cabin air filters) in Stage 2; brakes, suspension and exterior lighting stay out until Stage 4+. See `09-strategy/21`.
- **Biggest constraint:** growth is cash-limited. Inventory needs outpace monthly profit, so expansion is paced by the reinvestment rule and gates G1–G5.

## Changes to the brief's assumptions (with reasons)
| Brief said | Recommendation | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Trunk organizer as the #1 candidate | Moved to Stage 2, sourced outside China | ~42.6% duty on China-made 4202 goods [T6]; FORTEM has ~51k reviews; bulky shipping; weakest contribution in the model |
| $120 for samples | $85 samples + $60 micro-batch unlocked only by pre-orders | Selling 12 real kits teaches more than holding samples |
| No line for legal setup | Added a ~$25 DBA; deferred the $300 LLC | Texas requires an assumed-name filing to trade under a brand; the LLC fee equals the whole budget [L1] |
| Website as a core channel | Local direct sales + free storefront first; Shopify/Amazon at gates | No subscriptions in the $300 phase; local sales have the highest contribution |
