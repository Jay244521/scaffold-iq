# 03 — Ranked Product Opportunity Matrix

**The live, editable version is the `Opportunity Matrix` tab in [`../05-finance/operating-workbook.xlsx`](../05-finance/operating-workbook.xlsx).** Change any score or weight there and the rankings recalculate. This page is a snapshot.

## Method
Each product is scored 1–5 on 10 criteria, where a higher score is always better for us. Two weightings are applied:

| Criterion | Launch weight | Strategic weight |
|---|---|---|
| Demand evidence | 15 | 25 |
| Capital fit / MOQ | 15 | 0 |
| Unit margin potential | 15 | 20 |
| Shipping & storage simplicity | 10 | 0 |
| Low return / fitment risk | 10 | 5 |
| Low liability / regulatory risk | 10 | 5 |
| Differentiation potential | 10 | 20 |
| Competitive room | 5 | 5 |
| Bundle / ecosystem fit | 5 | 5 |
| Proprietary path | 5 | 15 |

**Overall = 60% Launch + 40% Strategic** while capital is $300. Shift the blend toward Strategic as capital grows.

**Honesty note:** scores are analyst judgements informed by the evidence column. They are not measured data. Demand scores in particular rely on review counts and category presence, because no reliable search-volume data was available (see the gaps section of `sources.md`). Re-score after customer interviews and supplier quotes.

## How to read the result
- **The launch lens and the strategic lens disagree.** That is the point. The products that build a durable brand (cargo protection system, custom liners, pet cargo liner) need capital we don't have. The products we can afford (microfiber, brushes, pet-hair tool) are commodities. **The plan uses the affordable products to earn the capital and customer data needed for the strategic ones.** See [`04-top-five-product-analysis.md`](04-top-five-product-analysis.md).
- **The trunk organizer slipped from the brief's #1 to #10.** Three reasons:
  1. Entrenched leaders with tens of thousands of reviews (FORTEM about 51k) [C1].
  2. About 42.6% duty if made in China [T6].
  3. Bulky FBA dimensions.

  It is still a good Stage-2 product if sourced outside China.
- **Products marked "avoid" carry safety or compatibility exposure** (seat covers and side airbags; phone mounts and falling devices; magnets and charging) or have weak local demand (frost covers in DFW).
