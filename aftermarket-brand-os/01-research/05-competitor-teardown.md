## 3. How the successful ones built catalogs and distribution
1. **They started with one hero product in one niche** (FORTEM organizer, Drop Stop gap filler, 4Knines liner, WeatherTech floor liner).
2. **They protected the brand early:** registered trademark → Amazon Brand Registry → A+ content and brand store (Lusso Gear filed its mark before scaling [C8]).
3. **They expanded into adjacent problems in the same space** (organizer → seat-back → cargo; liner → seat cover → hammock).
4. **They built a fitment database.** The premium brands (WeatherTech, Covercraft, 3D MAXpider, RealTruck) compete on *fit data*, not fabric. That data is the moat, and it builds up product by product, return by return.
5. **They added distribution once demand was proven:** dealers, installers and big-box retail followed online traction.

## 5. What we will not do
- Copy protected trade dress, designs, product names, logos or photography of any brand above.
- Use competitor trademarks in our listings, keywords or ads in a way that implies affiliation. Comparative advertising must be truthful and must be reviewed by counsel before use.
- Buy reviews, offer incentives for reviews, or use review-swap groups (see the review policy in the launch strategy).
