# Sources and Evidence Log

Research date: **2026-11-13**. How this was gathered: web search. This session's network blocked fetching pages directly, so each claim below rests on search-result text from the named page, not a full reading of it. Re-check anything marked *secondary* against the primary source before you rely on it for money or legal decisions.

**Evidence grades used across this folder**
- **V (verified-primary):** the figure comes from a government body or the company's own page.
- **S (secondary):** trade press, a law-firm alert, or a vendor or affiliate blog citing the primary source.
- **L (listing):** a marketplace or supplier listing. It shows an asking price or a claim, not a quote.
- **A (assumption):** our own planning estimate. It must be replaced with real data.
