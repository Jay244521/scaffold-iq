# 02 — Market Research Report

Evidence grades (V/S/L/A) and source IDs refer to [`sources.md`](sources.md).

## 1. Market size and shape
- **About $52.9B U.S. specialty-equipment market in 2025**, growing about 1% a year [M1, S]. This SEMA figure covers accessories and performance parts. Interior accessories for sedans and SUVs are only a slice of it, and **we have no verified size for that slice**. We have not estimated one, because any number would be invented.
- **Trucks and SUVs drive about 43% of spending** [M2, S]. Crossovers and SUVs are the largest passenger segment, which supports our sedan/crossover/SUV focus. Pickups remain RealTruck's core, so our segment overlaps with it less.
- **About 56% of purchases start online** [M3, S]. That supports a direct-to-consumer and marketplace model.
- **Flat overall growth means share is won from incumbents, not handed out by a rising tide.** A new brand wins on a sharper customer promise (fit, durability, easy returns), not on category growth.

## 3. Channel economics (what each sale costs us)
- **Amazon:** about 12–15% referral fee [P1], FBA fee by size tier, 3.5% fuel surcharge from April 2026, and a $39.99/mo Professional plan [P2]. Brand Registry (needed for A+ content and brand protection) requires a trademark [P2].
- **Shopify:** $39/mo plus 2.9% + 30¢ [P3]. Cheaper options exist for the validation phase (see [`../06-go-to-market/15-ecommerce-launch-strategy.md`](../06-go-to-market/15-ecommerce-launch-strategy.md)).
- **eBay Motors P&A:** about 13.6% plus a per-order fee [P4]. Free to list at low volume.

## 4. The 2026 import environment (critical for a private-label brand)
1. **IEEPA "reciprocal" tariffs are gone** (Supreme Court, Feb 2026) [T1]. The temporary Section 122 surcharge ran until 24 July 2026 [T2].
2. **Section 301 still applies to China-made goods**, and it varies by product [T3]:
   - Microfiber cloths (6307.10): **5.3% + 7.5% = about 12.8%** [T5, V]
   - Trunk organizers (4202.92): **17.6% + 25% = about 42.6%** [T6, V]. This single fact makes China-sourced trunk organizers much less attractive.
   - One source reports an additional 12.5% Section 301 duty since July 2026. This is **unverified**, so the model keeps a tariff buffer.
3. **De minimis is gone** for every origin [T4]. Even samples and small air shipments can incur duties and broker fees. Budget for this.
4. **What follows:** for 4202 bags and organizers, look at non-China origins (Vietnam, Cambodia, Pakistan, India), where only MFN duty applies. Microfiber from Pakistan avoids Section 301 entirely. **Confirm the HTS code and duty with a licensed customs broker before any import.**

## 5. DFW as the launch market
- Dallas–Fort Worth is a large, car-dependent metro with long commutes and summer heat. Heat stresses interiors and hardware. Sunshades, heat-resistant liners and interior care fit the local need. (Directional judgement **A**. Validate it in interviews.)
- **Local channels available at $0:** car meets (Cars & Coffee), Facebook Marketplace, Nextdoor, independent detailers, car washes, used-car dealers, and dealership accessory managers. Local hand-delivery removes shipping and marketplace fees. That is the highest-margin channel in the model (about 60–70% contribution).

## 6. Strategic conclusions
1. The category is large but mature. **Differentiate on fit, durability and service**, not on novelty.
2. **Capital is the binding constraint.** The opportunity matrix weights MOQ and capital fit heavily at launch.
3. **Pet owners are the sharpest wedge:** high spend, visible pain (hair, scratches, mess) and a natural bundle chain (care kit → pet-hair tool → cargo liner → full cargo protection system).
4. **Tariffs push us toward domestic wholesale for validation**, then non-China or low-duty origins at scale.
