# 15 — Ecommerce Launch Strategy

## 1. Channel comparison

## 2. Best first channel for a $300 business
**Local DFW direct sales plus a free one-page storefront**, with eBay as the first online marketplace.

**Why this beats Amazon on day 1:**
1. It costs $0 per month and earns the highest contribution.
2. It needs no reviews or trademark.
3. It gives the fastest customer learning (every sale is an interview).
4. Paid pre-orders fund the micro-batch.

Amazon comes after the trademark filing and proven demand, because Brand Registry protection and A+ content depend on it [P2].

## 3. Product listing template
```
TITLE (≤ 150 chars on Amazon; front-load the benefit):
[Brand] Microfiber Car Care Kit – 9-Piece Color-Coded Towels for Interior, Glass & Drying – Lint-Free, Scratch-Safe, Machine Washable

5 BULLETS:
1. COLOR-CODED SYSTEM – 3 colors for interior, glass and paint so dirt never crosses surfaces. Guide card included.
2. WHAT'S INSIDE – 6 all-purpose (16×16 in, [xxx] gsm), 1 large drying towel (20×30 in), 2 glass towels, mesh wash bag.
3. SCRATCH-SAFE – Edgeless [verify] plush that's safe on clear-coat and screens.
4. 15-MINUTE INTERIOR RESET – Scan the QR code for a step-by-step video made for everyday drivers.
5. [BRAND] PROMISE – Not right? Return it within 30 days. Questions answered by a real person in Texas.

DESCRIPTION: Problem → system → how to use → care instructions → guarantee.
BACKEND KEYWORDS: only accurate, non-trademarked terms (no competitor brand names).
```
Every claim (gsm, edgeless, lint-free) must be **verified on our samples** before publishing.

## 4. Product photography requirements (phone + daylight + white poster board, ~$0–$15)
1. Main image: product on pure white background, filling ≥85% of frame (Amazon main-image rule)
2. Contents flat-lay with labels
3. In-use: cleaning a real dashboard (DFW cars)
4. Before/after interior (same angle, same light)
5. Scale image (towel in hand / against a seat)
6. Colour-coding infographic (Canva free)
7. Guarantee and "Texas-tested" card. Only use this once we have actually run the tests.
8. Short 15–30 s video

**Specs:** ≥2,000 px on the long side, sRGB, JPEG.

## 5. Pricing strategy
- **Anchor:** $29.99 for the 9-piece kit sits below the Rag Company bundle's regular price ($97) and Chemical Guys' 12-pack regular price ($71.96), and in line with their sale prices ($40 / $45) [C3]. We compete on simplicity and the guide, not on rock-bottom price.
- **Local price:** same $29.99. Offer "2 kits for $50" to raise order value. The local margin supports it.
- **Pet Owner Kit:** $36.99 (+ pet-hair tool).
- **Wholesale:** $18 per kit at 10+ units; $16 at 50+ (check against the model).
- **Never** run fake "was/now" pricing. A reference price must be one we genuinely charged.

## 7. Customer acquisition plan (first 90 days)
1. **Weeks 1–4, warm network:** friends, family, coworkers, neighbourhood groups. Ask for pre-orders.
2. **Car meets (Cars & Coffee DFW events):** bring sample kits and a QR code to a pre-order form. Follow event and venue rules on selling.
3. **Facebook Marketplace, Nextdoor:** local listings with real photos.
4. **Dog community:** groomers, doggy day-cares, dog-park meetups, local dog Facebook groups (where allowed).
5. **B2B:** 10 detailer, car wash and used-car-lot visits with sample kits (email template G).
6. **Content:** 3 short videos a week (before/after resets).
7. **Paid test:** $25 boosted local post only after 5 organic sales. Measure cost per pre-order.

## 8. Customer service procedures
- **Response time:** within 12 business hours. One shared inbox and a phone number with business-hours voicemail.
- **Macros:** where's my order; how to use; fit question; return request; damaged item; wholesale inquiry.
- **Log every contact** in a simple sheet: date, issue, product, resolution, root cause. This becomes the product-improvement backlog and later the fitment database.

## 9. Returns and warranty procedures
- **Returns:** 30 days, unused or test-fit. Prepaid label for defects; customer-paid for change of mind on own-site orders. Amazon returns follow Amazon policy.
- **Warranty:** 1-year limited warranty against manufacturing defects for the Care Kit (stitching, fraying in normal use). **Have counsel review** the warranty wording. Written warranties on consumer products are governed by federal rules (Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act) once offered.
- **Process:** request → photo → resolution in 2 business days (refund, replace, or return-to-inspect) → log the root cause.

## 10. Review generation (platform-compliant)
- **Allowed:**
  - A neutral review request after delivery (Amazon's "Request a Review" button, or one neutral follow-up email on our own site)
  - Product inserts that ask for *honest* feedback **without** incentives, without asking only happy customers, and without directing unhappy customers elsewhere
  - Amazon Vine, once enrolled and eligible (Brand Registry)
- **Never:**
  - Pay for reviews, offer refunds or free products for reviews, or trade reviews
  - Ask friends or family to review on Amazon
  - Filter requests so only satisfied customers are asked (review gating)
  - Write fake reviews

The FTC finalised a rule banning fake and incentivised reviews in 2024. **Verify its current requirements with counsel.**
