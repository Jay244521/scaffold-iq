# 16 — Marketing Strategy

## Objective by stage
| Stage | Goal | Primary KPI |
|---|---|---|
| Validation (days 1–30) | Prove people pay | Paid pre-orders (target ≥ 8), interview count (≥ 15) |
| Launch (days 31–90) | Repeatable local and online sales | 25 orders/month; repeat rate; cost per order |
| Growth (G1–G3) | Marketplace scale + brand protection | 100 orders/month; Amazon conversion; contribution after ads |
| Brand (G4+) | Recognised name in pet and cargo protection | Branded search volume; wholesale accounts; review rating ≥ 4.5 |

## Positioning in one line
*Vehicle care and protection that fits right, survives Texas heat and is easy to return.*

## Audience priority
1. **DFW dog owners with SUVs or crossovers.** Sharpest pain, highest lifetime value (Care Kit → liner → system).
2. **Busy commuters.** Volume for the Care Kit.
3. **New-car buyers via dealers.** B2B volume.

## Channel playbook
| Channel | Tactic | Budget | Measure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Short video (TikTok, Reels, Shorts) | 15-second interior reset before/afters; dog-car mess fixes; "heat test" clips | $0 | Views → link clicks → orders |
| Local community | Car meets, dog events, Nextdoor, Marketplace | $0 | Pre-orders per event |
| Email/SMS (consented) | Launch list from survey; 90-day refill reminder; new-product announcements | $0 (free tier of an email tool) | Open/click; repeat rate |
| Partnerships | Groomers, detailers, dog day-cares: wholesale or referral code | Sample kits | Orders per partner |
| PR / local media | "DFW founder builds a car-care brand tested in Texas heat" pitch to local blogs and newsletters | $0 | Mentions; traffic |
| Paid (after proof) | $25 boosted local post → later Amazon Sponsored Products at ~15% of sales (TACoS) | Gated | Cost per order < contribution |
| SEO (website) | How-to guides: "how to remove dog hair from car carpet", "how to clean car interior in 15 minutes", vehicle fit guides | $0 | Organic visits |

## Content calendar (weekly rhythm)
- **Mon:** before/after reset video
- **Wed:** dog-car tip
- **Fri:** behind-the-brand (sample tests, supplier decisions; honest and transparent)
- **Weekend:** car-meet or local event presence

## Claims discipline (to protect the brand legally)
- Only make performance claims we have **tested and documented**: gsm weighed, lint test, heat test.
- Never claim "Made in USA" unless it meets the FTC "all or virtually all" standard.
- Never use competitor trademarks in ads or keywords in a way that suggests affiliation.
- Disclose any material connection with creators: free product or payment requires a "#ad" or similar disclosure (FTC Endorsement Guides).

## Budget gates
No paid spend beyond the approved $25 experiment until:
1. Contribution per order after ads is positive in the model.
2. The founder approves a new budget line.
