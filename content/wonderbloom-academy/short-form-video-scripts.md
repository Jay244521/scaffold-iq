# Wonderbloom Academy — Short-Form Video Scripts (TikTok / Reels)

**Audience:** Parents of 2–6 year olds
**Length:** 30 seconds each, vertical 9:16
**Format notes:** On-screen text (OST) should be readable with the sound off; keep every caption on screen for at least 1.5 seconds. Hook in the first 2 seconds. End each video with the book cover on screen and a clear call to action (CTA).

---

## Script 3 — "Satisfying Maze + Coloring Time-Lapse"

**Angle:** Pure satisfying ASMR-style visual — little narration, big payoff. Designed for high rewatch and saves.
**Vibe:** Overhead (top-down) locked-off camera, clean desk, natural light. Child's hands only. Sped-up footage with real-time "payoff" moments.

| Time | Visual | Audio | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:02 | Overhead: a blank maze page (Page 19, Professor Ollo's Treehouse). Small hand places a green crayon on the "S." | Crayon tap sound (ASMR). | **Watch her solve it 👀** |
| 0:02–0:10 | Time-lapse (4x): crayon winding through the maze. One wrong turn into a dead end — hand pauses, backs up, tries again. | Sped-up crayon scratching + soft music begins. | *Wrong turn…* → *backs up…* → *tries again* 💪 |
| 0:10–0:12 | Real-time: crayon reaches the "E" door. Tiny hand slaps the table. | Kid: "I DID IT!" | **SOLVED ✅** |
| 0:12–0:15 | Page flip (satisfying paper whoosh) to Color by Number (Page 24), all blank. | Paper flip sound. | *Now the fun part…* 🎨 |
| 0:15–0:24 | Time-lapse (8x): the garden fills with color — blue sky, yellow sun, green grass, orange carrots, pink flowers. Fixed overhead shot so the page "blooms" into color. | Music builds, crayon sounds layered. | *Blue… green… orange… pink…* |
| 0:24–0:27 | Real-time: hand lifts away to reveal the finished page. Slow push-in. | Music hits a soft "ding" moment. | **From blank → bloom** 🌸 |
| 0:27–0:30 | Stack of finished pages beside the closed book; hand places the graduation certificate on top. | VO (parent, gentle): "Wonderbloom Academy. Link in bio." | **32 pages of this. Ages 2–6. Link in bio.** |

**Caption:** The backing-up-from-the-dead-end part 🥹 Problem-solving in real time. Wait for the color reveal 🌈 #satisfyingvideo #toddleractivities #kidsmaze #colorbynumber #screenfreeplay #preschoolactivities
**Audio:** Trending "satisfying"/lo-fi instrumental; keep natural crayon ASMR audible over it.

## Production Checklist (all three videos)

- [ ] Hook visible within 2 seconds (movement + text)
- [ ] Captions burned in (sound-off viewers)
- [ ] Book cover appears at least twice (mid-video + end)
- [ ] Only show children's hands or backs of heads if privacy is a concern; get guardian consent for any child who appears
- [ ] End frame holds the cover for 2+ seconds
- [ ] Post the same video to TikTok and Reels, but swap in each platform's trending audio
- [ ] A/B test the hooks: Script 1 (pain point), Script 2 (curiosity), Script 3 (visual satisfaction)
