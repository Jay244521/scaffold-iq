# Wonderbloom Academy — Interior Activity Pages

**Format:** 32-page activity book (8.5" × 11" recommended, single-sided activity pages)
**Ages:** 2–6, in three difficulty bands marked on each page with Professor Ollo's feather:

| Band | Icon | Ages | Skills |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sprout | 🪶 | 2–3 | Big-motor tracing, counting to 5, matching, 2-path mazes |
| Bloom | 🪶🪶 | 4–5 | Pencil control, counting to 10, patterns, grid mazes |
| Blossom | 🪶🪶🪶 | 5–6 | Letters/numbers, simple addition, longer mazes |

**Cast**
- **Benny** — a cautious bunny who likes to go slow and check twice. Catchphrase: *"Slow and steady, I'm ready!"*
- **Luna** — a clever fox who loves clues and patterns. Catchphrase: *"I spy a clue!"*
- **Zippy** — an energetic robot who goes *zip-zip-ZOOM*. Catchphrase: *"Beep-boop, let's GO!"*
- **Professor Ollo** — a wise owl teacher with round glasses. Catchphrase: *"Whooo's ready to learn?"*

**Layout notes for the illustrator/designer**
- Tracing lines: dashed, 4–6 mm thick for Sprout, 2–3 mm for Bloom/Blossom. Always mark a green dot ● for "start" and a small arrow showing direction.
- Mazes: paths at least 12 mm wide for Sprout, 8 mm for Bloom, 6 mm for Blossom. `S` = start, `E` = end, `#` = wall. Each ASCII maze below is drawn to scale on a square grid (one character = one grid square).
- Keep one short instruction line per page (read aloud by a grown-up) plus one character speech bubble.

## SECTION 1: TRACING TRAIL (Pages 4–9)

### PAGE 4 — Benny's Straight Hops 🪶

**Instruction:** Help Benny hop to the carrots. Trace each line from left to right.
**Layout:** 4 horizontal dashed lines, each starting at a Benny icon (green dot ●) and ending at a carrot.
- Line 1: long straight line → 1 carrot
- Line 2: long straight line → 2 carrots
- Line 3: long straight line → 3 carrots
- Line 4: long straight line → 4 carrots

**Bonus row:** 4 vertical dashed lines top-to-bottom: "Benny drops carrots into his basket."

**Benny:** "Slow and steady, I'm ready!"

### PAGE 5 — Zippy's Zig-Zag Zoom 🪶

**Instruction:** Trace Zippy's lightning-fast zig-zags. Zip! Zap!
**Layout:** 3 zig-zag dashed lines running left to right from Zippy to a charging plug.
- Line 1: 3 big peaks (tall, wide)
- Line 2: 5 medium peaks
- Line 3: 7 small peaks (Bloom challenge)

**Sequence to say aloud while tracing:** "Up — down — up — down — ZOOM!"

**Zippy:** "Beep-boop, let's GO!"

### PAGE 6 — Luna's Swishy Waves 🪶🪶

**Instruction:** Luna swishes her tail. Trace the waves from Luna to her den.
**Layout:**
- Row 1: 3 big "hill" curves (∩∩∩)
- Row 2: 4 "bowl" curves (∪∪∪∪)
- Row 3: continuous wave (∿∿∿∿) — 5 waves
- Row 4: loop-de-loops (ℓℓℓℓ) — 5 loops (Blossom challenge, prepares for cursive "l" and "e")

**Luna:** "Swish, swish! Keep your pencil on the paper."

### PAGE 7 — Professor Ollo's Circles & Shapes 🪶🪶

**Instruction:** Trace the shapes in Professor Ollo's classroom.
**Layout (each shape traced 3 times, big → medium → small):**
1. ◯ Circle — "Ollo's glasses" (start at the top, go around counter-clockwise)
2. □ Square — "a window" (start top-left, go right → down → left → up)
3. △ Triangle — "a slice of pie" (start at the top point, slide down right, across, back up)
4. ★ Star (Blossom only) — dotted with numbered corners 1–5 so the child follows the order: 1 (top) → 2 (bottom-left) → 3 (far right) → 4 (far left) → 5 (bottom-right) → back to 1

**Ollo:** "Whooo can spot a circle in your room?"

### PAGE 8 — Letter Trail: A · B · C 🪶🪶🪶

**Instruction:** Trace the letters. Then color the picture that starts with that letter.

| Letter | Trace (dashed) | Stroke order | Picture |
|---|---|---|---|
| A a | 3 uppercase, 3 lowercase | A: slant down-left, slant down-right, across the middle · a: circle, then short line down on the right | 🍎 Apple (held by Benny) |
| B b | 3 uppercase, 3 lowercase | B: line down, bump, bump · b: tall line down, circle at the bottom-right | ⚽ Ball (kicked by Zippy) |
| C c | 3 uppercase, 3 lowercase | C: start at top-right, curve around to bottom-right | 🐱 Cat (reading with Luna) |

**Bottom row:** "Trace your favorite friend's first letter:" **B** (Benny) · **L** (Luna) · **Z** (Zippy) · **O** (Ollo), each dashed and large.

### PAGE 9 — Number Trail: 1 to 5 🪶🪶

**Instruction:** Trace each number. Count the pictures. Say the number out loud!

| Number | Trace | Stroke tip (rhyme) | Pictures to count |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 1 1 1 | "Straight line down, and then we're done — that's the way to make a 1." | 1 owl |
| 2 | 2 2 2 2 | "Around and back on the railroad track — 2, 2, 2!" | 2 carrots |
| 3 | 3 3 3 3 | "Around the tree, around the tree — that's the way to make a 3." | 3 gears |
| 4 | 4 4 4 4 | "Down and over and down some more — that's the way to make a 4." | 4 leaves |
| 5 | 5 5 5 5 | "Down, around, then put on a hat — 5 is fat!" | 5 stars |

### PAGE 16 — Benny's Garden Path (Choose-a-Path Maze) 🪶

**Instruction:** Benny is cautious! Help him pick the safe path to the carrot garden.

**Layout:** Benny sits at the bottom-center (START). Three wide, curvy paths spread upward like a fan:
- **Path 1 (left):** Curves up and left → ends at a **big muddy puddle** 💧 (dead end; Benny says "Too splashy!")
- **Path 2 (middle):** Wiggles up with 2 gentle S-curves, passes a friendly butterfly → ends at the **carrot garden gate** 🥕 (END)
- **Path 3 (right):** Curves up and right → ends at a **prickly bush** 🌵 (dead end; "Too pokey!")

Paths are 12 mm+ wide, clearly separated, and never cross.

**Benny:** "Slow and steady — which way is safe?"
